Support

Mantle: Become Unshakeable · bodyweight strength, no gear

Mantle is a native iPhone and Apple Watch training app developed by Andrew Parrish. There is no Mantle account to sign into and no Mantle server holding your workouts.

Contact

Email is the fastest way to reach a person. Use it for app issues, feedback, and privacy or deletion requests.

aparrish83@gmail.com

Please include your device model, iOS or watchOS version, and what you were doing when something went wrong. Typical reply time is within a few business days.

You can also open a GitHub issue if you already have a GitHub account. Email does not require one.

Purchases

  • The Subterranean Pass (monthly and annual auto-renewable subscriptions) and the Lifetime Pass (one-time non-consumable) are sold through Apple In-App Purchase.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID. Manage, turn off auto-renew, or cancel in iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • In Mantle, use Restore Purchases on the Subterranean Pass paywall if a device lost its entitlement (for example after reinstalling or switching iPhones).
  • Mantle does not revoke a successful purchase. Refunds, if any, are handled by Apple under Apple’s terms.

Health & iCloud

  • Apple Health is optional. Turn Save to Health on in the Rig (Settings). Revoke anytime in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.
  • iCloud sync is optional. It uses your private CloudKit database so Mantle app records can move across your devices. You can leave sync off.

Delete your data

Mantle has no account and no developer-operated cloud of your workouts. To remove data:

  • Delete the app to remove on-device storage on that device.
  • Turn off iCloud sync in Mantle, then manage or delete remaining CloudKit data in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud (or by deleting the app’s iCloud data where Apple provides that control).
  • Delete Health workouts in the Health app if you previously enabled Save to Health.
  • Email aparrish83@gmail.com if you need help with a deletion request. Because we do not host your training data on our servers, most deletion is done with the device, Health, iCloud, and App Store controls above.
