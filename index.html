Support

Mantle: Become Unshakeable · bodyweight strength, no gear

Mantle is a native iPhone and Apple Watch training app. There is no account to sign into and no server of ours holding your workouts.

Get help

github.com/Flam1ngFir3ball/mantle-privacy/issues

Replace this with your support email when you have one, then update this page and App Store Connect Contact Information.

Purchases

  • Subscriptions and the Lifetime Pass are sold through Apple. Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • In Mantle, use Restore Purchases on the Subterranean Pass paywall if a device lost its entitlement.
  • Nothing you bought is taken back after a successful purchase.

Health & iCloud

  • Apple Health: turn Save to Health on in the Rig (Settings). Revoke anytime in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access.
  • iCloud sync uses your private CloudKit database so training can move across your devices. You can leave sync off.
