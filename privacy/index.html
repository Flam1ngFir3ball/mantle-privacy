MANTLE Support Privacy Terms

Privacy Policy

Effective: September 8, 2026 · Last updated: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy is provided by Andrew Parrish, the independent developer of Mantle (“we,” “us”), to explain what information is involved when you use Mantle on iPhone and Apple Watch. It is written to meet Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1, including identification of data, third-party handling, retention, deletion, and how you can revoke consent.

1. Summary

  • No Mantle account and no Mantle server. We do not operate a backend that receives your workouts.
  • Training data lives on your devices.
  • Optional sync uses your Apple iCloud (private CloudKit database under your Apple ID).
  • Optional Apple Health and Photos access are for features you turn on.
  • Purchases go through Apple StoreKit.
  • We do not sell data, show ads, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We do not use health or fitness data for advertising, marketing, or use-based data mining.

2. Information on your device

Mantle stores workout logs, dig-site progress, settings, optional companion name, relics, and related app state in on-device storage so the app works offline. That information is created by you using the app (or generated by the app from your training) and is processed on the device to run Mantle.

If you create a custom dig site (“claim”), the text you type (name, exercise notes, and similar) stays in that store and, if sync is on, in your private iCloud database.

This on-device store is how Mantle functions. Paid features are not conditioned on granting Health, Photos, notification, or iCloud permission.

3. Apple services you may use

These are Apple platforms. Apple’s own terms and privacy policy also apply to iCloud, Health, Photos, notifications, and App Store purchases. When Mantle uses those services, Apple is the third party that processes the relevant data. Apple provides protection of that data under Apple’s policies and the App Store Review Guidelines. We do not share Mantle data with analytics tools, advertising networks, other third-party SDKs, or related companies, because we do not use those products and we do not operate a parent or subsidiary that receives user data.

  • iCloud / CloudKit (optional): When sync is enabled, Mantle syncs Mantle app records (for example session logs, settings, claims, relics, and progress) through your Apple ID’s private CloudKit database so your devices can stay in step. We do not operate a separate cloud backend for that content, and we do not have a developer server that reads your private CloudKit database.
  • Apple Health / HealthKit (optional): With permission, Mantle can save completed bodyweight sessions to Apple Health as traditional strength-training workouts and related active energy. On Apple Watch, during a session you start there, Mantle may also read heart rate and active energy to show live feedback and to save the workout. Mantle does not read clinical health records. Specific health data involved: workout type and duration, active energy, and (on Apple Watch sessions) heart rate. That data is used only to provide the workout experience to you. Mantle does not sell Health data and does not disclose it to third parties other than writing to Apple Health when you enable Save to Health.
  • Health data and iCloud: Mantle does not store HealthKit samples (heart rate, active energy, or Health workouts) in iCloud through Mantle’s CloudKit container. Optional Mantle iCloud sync is limited to Mantle app records described above, not HealthKit samples. If you enable Health in iCloud, Apple may sync Health data as part of Apple Health; that is Apple’s service.
  • Photos (optional, add-only): If you save a watch wallpaper from Mantle, the image is written to your photo library with add-only permission. Mantle does not need full photo library read access for that feature.
  • Notifications (optional): Local reminders (for example training day or return-after-quiet reminders) are scheduled on your device after you allow notifications. We do not operate a push-notification server for those reminders.
  • App Store / StoreKit: Subscription and Lifetime Pass purchases, restores, and entitlements are handled by Apple. We do not receive your full payment card details. Entitlement state needed to unlock paid features is checked through Apple’s purchase APIs on your device.
  • WatchConnectivity: The iPhone and Apple Watch apps exchange session plans and results between your paired devices.

4. What we do not collect

  • No sign-in with email, Google, or social networks, and no Mantle user account.
  • No advertising SDKs and no App Tracking Transparency tracking for ads.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not track you for advertising, and we do not operate a separate analytics product on top of Mantle.
  • We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. How information is used

On-device and (if enabled) iCloud-synced Mantle app records are used only to run Mantle: prescribe sessions, show progress, sync across your devices, optional Health logging, optional wallpaper save, and purchase unlock state. Health and fitness data is used only to provide that benefit to you (logging a session you completed, or live Watch feedback). It is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling, or data mining.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not share training content with data brokers or advertisers. Data may leave your device only through Apple services you enable (iCloud, Health, Photos, App Store) or if you export or screenshot it yourself.

We may disclose information if required by law. In practice, because we do not host your workouts on our servers, there is no Mantle-held training database to produce.

7. Retention, deletion, and withdrawing consent

You can revoke optional permissions and delete data as follows:

  • Delete the app to remove Mantle’s on-device store on that device.
  • iCloud: Turn sync off in Mantle. Copies in your private CloudKit database remain under your Apple ID until you delete them via Mantle (where offered), iCloud settings, or by managing CloudKit/iCloud data with Apple.
  • Health: Revoke access in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Mantle. Health workouts remain in Apple Health until you delete them in the Health app.
  • Photos: Revoke add-only access in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos. Wallpapers already saved stay in your library until you delete them.
  • Notifications: Revoke in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Mantle, or disable reminders in the app.
  • Purchases: Purchase history remains with Apple. Cancel auto-renew in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Because Mantle does not create an account, there is no in-app account-deletion flow. To request help deleting data, email aparrish83@gmail.com. We will explain the device, Health, iCloud, and App Store controls that apply; we cannot remotely wipe a private CloudKit database or Apple Health store that only you and Apple control.

8. Children’s privacy

Mantle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it to the extent we hold it. Mantle is not in the App Store Kids Category.

9. International users

Processing happens on your devices and, when you enable iCloud, in Apple’s infrastructure for your Apple ID region. If you are in the EU/EEA, UK, California, or another region with privacy rights, you can exercise access or deletion primarily by using the controls above (device, Health, iCloud, App Store) and by contacting us as below. We do not maintain a separate profile of you on our servers.

10. California “Do Not Sell”

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are commonly used under California law.

11. App Store privacy details

The App Privacy information on Mantle’s App Store product page should match these practices: we do not track; we do not collect data onto developer-controlled servers; optional Health, Photos, notifications, StoreKit, and iCloud are Apple services you control. Confirm the nutrition label in App Store Connect against the shipping binary and Mantle’s privacy manifest.

12. Changes

We may update this policy when Mantle’s practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Material changes will be reflected on this page before they apply. Continued use of Mantle after an update means the revised policy describes the then-current practices; it is not a substitute for permissions you grant or withdraw on the device.

Privacy questions and deletion requests: aparrish83@gmail.com. You can also use the Support page.
